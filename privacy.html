This Privacy Policy explains how Bejab ("Bejab", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Bejab tarot card journal on iPhone and iPad. Bejab is a private reflection and journaling tool. It does not predict outcomes and makes no guarantees about any result.

I

What stays on your device

Your private journal

Everything you create in Bejab is stored only inside the app's private container on your device:

  • Reading entries: card name, question, card meaning notes, reflection text, and dates.
  • Card photos you take with the camera or import from your photo library, plus their cropped working copies.
  • Reading pages (PNG images) that you choose to generate.
  • Your Sheet Credit balance, credit history, and the IDs of verified App Store purchases.
  • Light preferences such as whether you finished the first-run introduction.
Bejab has no account system, no server, and no cloud sync. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your entries, photos, voice, or pages.
II

Camera

Only for Scan Card

Bejab uses the camera only when you tap Scan Card, to photograph a physical tarot card. Card-name detection runs on your device using Apple's Vision framework. The detected name is shown to you and is written only after you confirm it.

III

Photos

Import & Save Image

Bejab accesses your photo library only when you tap Import Photo to add a card photo you already took, or when you tap Save Image to add a generated reading page to your library. Saving uses add-only access. Bejab never modifies your original photos.

IV

Microphone & speech recognition

Only for Speak Reflection

Bejab uses the microphone only when you tap Speak Reflection. Your speech is turned into text so you can review it; the text is written into your reflection only after you confirm it. Bejab does not store audio recordings.

Speech recognition is provided by Apple and may be processed on device or by Apple according to Apple's own privacy policy.
V

In-app purchases

Sheet Credits via the App Store

Sheet Credits are consumable purchases processed by Apple through the App Store. Bejab receives only a signed transaction from Apple (product ID and transaction ID) and never sees your payment details. Credits are stored on this device and cannot be restored after the app is removed or its data is cleared.

VI

Log Out, Delete Account & Clear All Data

You stay in control
  • Log Out ends the local session on this device and keeps your entries, pages, and credits.
  • Delete Account and Clear All Data permanently erase all readings, card photos, generated pages, cache, example history, purchase records, and your remaining credits from this device.

This cannot be undone.

VII

Children

Not directed to under-13s

Bejab is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

VIII

Changes

Always dated

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address and inside the app with a new effective date.
